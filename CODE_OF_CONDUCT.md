# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal data, or private communications.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect user security, project scope, or legal compliance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential disclosure, and attempts to use this project to evade service limits are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
